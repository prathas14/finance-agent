# Stocks and ETFs

## What Is a Stock?
A stock represents fractional ownership of a company. When the company profits, so do shareholders — via price appreciation and dividends.

## How to Evaluate a Stock
- **P/E Ratio**: Price ÷ Earnings per share. S&P 500 average is ~20-25x.
- **EPS (Earnings Per Share)**: Net income ÷ shares outstanding.
- **Market Cap**: Small-cap (<$2B), Mid-cap ($2-10B), Large-cap (>$10B).
- **Dividend Yield**: Annual dividend ÷ stock price.

## What Is an ETF?
An ETF (Exchange-Traded Fund) holds a basket of securities and trades like a stock. Benefits:
- Instant diversification
- Low expense ratios (e.g. VTI = 0.03%)
- Tax efficiency
- Liquidity

## Popular ETFs
| ETF | Tracks | Expense Ratio |
|-----|--------|---------------|
| VTI | Total US Market | 0.03% |
| SPY | S&P 500 | 0.09% |
| BND | Total Bond Market | 0.03% |
| QQQ | Nasdaq-100 | 0.20% |
| VEA | Developed Markets ex-US | 0.05% |

## Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA)
Invest a fixed amount (e.g. $500/month) regardless of price. This removes emotion and reduces the impact of volatility over time.
