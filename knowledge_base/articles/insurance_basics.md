# Insurance Basics

## Why Insurance Exists
Insurance protects against low-probability, high-impact financial events. The math: pay a small certain cost (premium) to avoid a potentially catastrophic uncertain loss. You're pooling risk with many others.

**Rule of thumb**: Insure risks you cannot afford to bear yourself. Self-insure (skip insurance) for risks whose worst case you can absorb.

## Types of Personal Insurance

### Life Insurance
Replaces your income for dependents if you die. You need it if people depend on your income.

**Term life insurance**: Coverage for a set period (10, 20, 30 years). Pure insurance. Low cost. Correct choice for most people.
- Rule of thumb: 10–12× your annual income in coverage.
- $500,000 20-year term for healthy 30-year-old: ~$25/month.

**Whole/Universal life insurance**: Permanent coverage with a cash value component. Much more expensive. Conflates insurance and investment — almost always worse than buying term + investing the difference. Avoid unless estate planning needs are sophisticated.

### Disability Insurance
Most people are more likely to become disabled than to die during their working years. Replaces income if you cannot work.

**Short-term disability**: Covers 60-180 days. Often employer-provided.
**Long-term disability**: Covers years or until retirement. Most important. Look for "own occupation" definition — pays if you can't do YOUR specific job.

Employer plans cover 60% of salary (taxable if employer-paid). Consider supplementing.

### Health Insurance
In the US: most people get through employer, government (Medicare/Medicaid), or marketplace (ACA). Understand:
- **Deductible**: You pay first before insurance kicks in.
- **Copay**: Fixed amount per visit.
- **Coinsurance**: Percentage you pay after deductible.
- **Out-of-pocket maximum**: Most you pay in one year.
- **Network**: In-network providers cost less.

HDHP + HSA combination is financially optimal for healthy, younger workers.

### Auto Insurance
Legally required in most states. Components:
- **Liability**: Pays others' damages if you cause accident. Required. Carry enough to protect your assets.
- **Collision**: Repairs your car after accident. Required by lender.
- **Comprehensive**: Non-collision damage (theft, weather, animal). Required by lender.
- **Uninsured/underinsured motorist**: Protects you if at-fault driver has no insurance.

Drop collision/comprehensive on old cars whose value is less than a few years of premiums.

### Homeowners/Renters Insurance
**Homeowners**: Required by mortgage lender. Covers structure and belongings. Understand: replacement cost vs. actual cash value. Keep an inventory.

**Renters insurance**: Covers your belongings (not the structure). Very cheap (~$15-30/month). Essential — your landlord's insurance doesn't cover your stuff.

### Umbrella Insurance
Extends liability coverage beyond home and auto limits. $1 million in coverage for ~$150-300/year. Essential once your net worth exceeds $500,000. Protects against large lawsuits.

## What Not to Insure
- Extended warranties: Almost never worth the cost.
- Credit card payment protection: Expensive relative to coverage.
- Flight insurance: Redundant with credit card travel benefits.
- Pet insurance: Often worth doing the math — can make sense for breeds with known health issues.
