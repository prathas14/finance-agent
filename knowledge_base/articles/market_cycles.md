# Market Cycles and Economic Cycles

## The Business Cycle
The economy moves through repeating phases. Understanding where we are in the cycle helps set return expectations, though timing the market is still nearly impossible.

### Four Phases of the Business Cycle

**1. Expansion**
- GDP growing, employment rising, consumer confidence high.
- Stocks: Generally rising. Growth stocks and cyclicals outperform.
- Bonds: Often underperform (rising rates expected).
- Duration: Typically the longest phase.

**2. Peak**
- Maximum economic output. Inflation rising. Central bank starts raising rates.
- Stocks: Late-cycle leaders: energy, materials, financials.
- Risk of overheating increasing.

**3. Contraction (Recession)**
- GDP shrinking for 2+ consecutive quarters (official definition).
- Unemployment rising, consumer spending falls.
- Stocks: Fall 20-40% on average in recessions. Defensive sectors (healthcare, utilities, consumer staples) outperform.
- Bonds: Rally as rates fall (Fed cuts rates to stimulate economy).

**4. Trough**
- Economic bottom. Central bank at maximum stimulus. Sentiment at worst.
- Often the best time to invest in stocks (but hardest emotionally).
- Recovery begins here.

## Bear and Bull Markets

### Bull Market
Rise of 20%+ from a recent low. Average duration: ~3.8 years. Average gain: ~175%.

### Bear Market
Fall of 20%+ from a recent high. Average duration: ~9.6 months. Average loss: ~36%.

US market history: Bear markets happen roughly every 3-4 years on average. They are normal, temporary, and always have been followed by recovery.

## Market Corrections
A correction is a 10%+ decline from a recent high. More common than bear markets — happen roughly once per year on average. Typically recover in 3-4 months.

## Historical US Market Downturns
| Event | Peak to Trough | Recovery Time |
|-------|---------------|---------------|
| 1929 Great Depression | -89% | 25 years |
| 1973-74 Oil Crisis | -48% | 7 years |
| 2000-02 Dot-com bust | -49% | 7 years |
| 2007-09 Financial Crisis | -57% | 5 years |
| 2020 COVID Crash | -34% | 5 months |
| 2022 Rate Hike Bear | -25% | ~2 years |

## Sectoral Rotation
Different sectors outperform at different cycle phases:

| Phase | Typically Outperforms |
|-------|--------------------|
| Early expansion | Financials, consumer discretionary |
| Mid expansion | Tech, industrials |
| Late expansion | Energy, materials |
| Recession | Utilities, healthcare, consumer staples |

## Why You Shouldn't Time the Market
- Missing the 10 best days in the S&P 500 over 20 years cuts your return roughly in half.
- The best and worst days cluster — if you sell during panic, you often miss the recovery.
- Professionals with full-time research teams fail to consistently time markets.
- The correct strategy: stay invested through cycles with an age-appropriate allocation.
