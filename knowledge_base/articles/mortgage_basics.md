# Mortgage Basics

## What Is a Mortgage?
A mortgage is a loan secured by real property. The lender provides funds to buy a home; you repay with interest over time. If you default, the lender can foreclose (take) the property.

## Key Mortgage Components
- **Principal**: The amount borrowed.
- **Interest rate**: The cost of borrowing, expressed annually.
- **Term**: Loan duration (15, 20, or 30 years most common).
- **Monthly payment**: Principal + interest + escrow (taxes and insurance).
- **Amortization**: How payments are split between principal and interest over time.

## Amortization
Early payments are mostly interest; later payments are mostly principal. On a 30-year $400,000 mortgage at 7%:
- Month 1 payment: ~$2,661 total ($2,333 interest, $328 principal)
- Year 15 payment: $2,661 total (~$1,650 interest, $1,011 principal)
- Final year: Nearly all principal.

This is why paying extra early has an outsized impact.

## Fixed vs. Adjustable Rate Mortgages (ARM)

### Fixed-Rate Mortgage
Interest rate locked for the life of the loan. Predictable payments. Protection from rising rates. Preferred in low-rate environments.

### Adjustable-Rate Mortgage (ARM)
Rate fixed for an initial period (e.g. 5 years), then adjusts annually based on a benchmark index + margin.

Named format: 5/1 ARM = fixed for 5 years, adjusts annually after.

Pros: Lower initial rate. Better if you plan to sell before adjustment.
Cons: Payment uncertainty. Risk of sharp increase at adjustment.

## Loan Types

### Conventional Loans
Not government-backed. Require 20% down to avoid PMI. Conforming loans meet Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac limits ($766,550 in 2024 for most areas).

### FHA Loans
Federal Housing Administration backed. Only 3.5% down with 580+ credit score. Requires mortgage insurance premium (MIP) for life of loan if <10% down.

### VA Loans
For veterans and active military. 0% down payment. No PMI. Very favorable terms.

### USDA Loans
For rural properties. 0% down for qualifying areas and income levels.

## PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance)
Required on conventional loans with <20% down. Protects the lender (not you). Typically 0.5–1.5% of loan amount annually. Removed once you reach 20% equity (must request it at 80% LTV).

## Interest Rate vs. APR
- **Interest rate**: The base borrowing cost.
- **APR (Annual Percentage Rate)**: Includes fees (origination, points, etc.). More accurate total cost comparison.

## Mortgage Points
Paying "points" upfront reduces your interest rate. 1 point = 1% of loan amount = ~0.25% rate reduction.

Break-even: $4,000 point cost / $100/month savings = 40 months to break even. Only worth it if you keep the loan that long.

## 15-Year vs. 30-Year Mortgage
| Feature | 15-Year | 30-Year |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Monthly payment | Higher | Lower |
| Total interest paid | Much less | Much more |
| Rate | ~0.5-0.75% lower | Standard |
| Equity build | Fast | Slow |
| Flexibility | Less (higher payment) | More |

## Prepaying Your Mortgage
Every extra dollar of principal payment saves future interest. On a $400,000 30-year mortgage at 7%, paying $200/month extra saves ~$100,000 in interest and pays off in ~24 years.

But: compare to investing that $200. If investments earn more than your mortgage rate after taxes, investing may win.
