# TIPS and I-Bonds: Inflation-Protected Investments

## Why Inflation Protection Matters
Fixed-rate investments lose real value when inflation rises. A 3% bond in a 5% inflation environment delivers a -2% real return. Inflation-protected securities solve this problem.

## TIPS (Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities)

### How TIPS Work
- Issued by the US Treasury.
- Principal adjusts up or down with CPI (Consumer Price Index) changes.
- Coupon rate is fixed, but applied to the adjusted principal.

Example: $10,000 TIPS with 1% coupon, inflation 4%:
- Principal becomes $10,400
- Annual interest = 1% × $10,400 = $104
- At maturity, you receive the inflation-adjusted principal (or original, whichever is greater — deflation protection).

### TIPS Maturities
Available in 5, 10, and 30-year maturities. Trade on secondary market like regular Treasuries.

### Real Yield
TIPS offer a "real yield" — the return above inflation. When real yields are negative, TIPS still preserve purchasing power but don't grow in real terms.

### Tax Considerations
Phantom income problem: The principal increase each year is taxable even though you don't receive it until maturity. Better held in tax-advantaged accounts (IRA, 401k).

### TIPS ETFs
- TIP (iShares TIPS Bond ETF): ~0.19% expense ratio, broad TIPS exposure.
- VTIP (Vanguard Short-Term Inflation-Protected): Shorter duration, less interest rate risk.

## I-Bonds (Series I Savings Bonds)

### How I-Bonds Work
- Issued directly by the US Treasury (TreasuryDirect.gov).
- Earn a composite rate = fixed rate + inflation adjustment (based on CPI-U, updated every 6 months in May and November).
- Tax-deferred federal taxes; exempt from state/local taxes.

### Key Rules
- Maximum purchase: $10,000/year per person (plus $5,000 via tax refund).
- Must hold at least 12 months before redeeming.
- Redeeming within 5 years forfeits the last 3 months of interest.
- No secondary market — only buy/sell from Treasury.
- Matures in 30 years.

### Best Use Cases
- Emergency fund (after 1-year lock-up period)
- Conservative savings for a 2-5 year goal
- Inflation hedge for money you won't need immediately

### I-Bond vs. TIPS Comparison
| Feature | I-Bond | TIPS |
|---------|--------|------|
| Max purchase | $10,000/year | No limit |
| Liquidity | Annual lock-up | Daily (via market) |
| Phantom income | No | Yes |
| Deflation protection | Yes (0% floor) | Yes (principal floor) |
| Availability | TreasuryDirect only | Brokerage/ETF |
