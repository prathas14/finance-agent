# Investing Basics

## Why Invest?
Investing allows your money to grow over time through compound interest and market returns. Keeping all your savings in cash means losing purchasing power to inflation (~3% per year).

## Asset Classes
- **Stocks**: Ownership in companies. Higher risk, higher long-term return (~7-10% annually historically).
- **Bonds**: Loans to governments or corporations. Lower risk, lower return (~2-5%).
- **ETFs**: Baskets of stocks/bonds trading on exchanges. Great for diversification at low cost.
- **Real Estate**: Property investment. Can provide rental income and appreciation.
- **Commodities**: Gold, oil, agricultural products. Often used as inflation hedges.

## The Power of Compound Interest
If you invest $10,000 at 7% annual return:
- After 10 years: ~$19,672
- After 20 years: ~$38,697
- After 30 years: ~$76,123

Starting early is the single most powerful thing you can do.

## Risk vs. Return
Higher potential returns come with higher risk. A diversified portfolio balances risk across asset classes based on your time horizon and risk tolerance.

## Key Principles
1. Start early — time in market beats timing the market.
2. Diversify — don't put all eggs in one basket.
3. Keep costs low — prefer index funds with low expense ratios.
4. Stay the course — don't panic-sell during downturns.
5. Rebalance annually — maintain your target allocation.
