# Net Worth Tracking

## What Is Net Worth?
Net worth = Total assets - Total liabilities.

It is the single most important number in personal finance. Tracking it over time shows whether you're building wealth or losing ground — regardless of income.

## Assets (What You Own)
### Liquid Assets
- Checking and savings accounts
- Money market accounts
- Brokerage accounts (stocks, bonds, ETFs)
- Cash value of life insurance

### Retirement Assets
- 401(k), 403(b), 457 balances
- IRA (Traditional and Roth) balances
- Pension present value

### Real Assets
- Home value (use current market estimate, not purchase price)
- Rental properties
- Vehicles (market value, not what you paid)
- Jewelry, collectibles, business ownership

## Liabilities (What You Owe)
- Mortgage balance(s)
- Auto loan balances
- Student loan balances
- Credit card balances
- Personal loans
- Medical debt
- Any other outstanding obligations

## Calculating Net Worth: Example
| Asset | Value | Liability | Balance |
|-------|-------|-----------|---------|
| Checking | $5,000 | Credit card | $2,000 |
| Brokerage | $45,000 | Auto loan | $8,000 |
| 401(k) | $80,000 | Mortgage | $280,000 |
| Home | $380,000 | Student loan | $15,000 |
| Car | $18,000 | | |
| **Total Assets** | **$528,000** | **Total Liabilities** | **$305,000** |
| | | **Net Worth** | **$223,000** |

## Net Worth Benchmarks
These are rough guidelines, not requirements:

| Age | Median Net Worth (US) | Recommended Target |
|-----|----------------------|-------------------|
| 25 | ~$14,000 | 0.5× annual income |
| 35 | ~$76,000 | 2× annual income |
| 45 | ~$168,000 | 4× annual income |
| 55 | ~$250,000 | 7× annual income |
| 65 | ~$266,000 | 10-12× annual income |

The Millionaire Next Door rule of thumb: Net worth target = (Age × Gross annual income) / 10.

## How Often to Track
- Monthly: Good habit, shows momentum.
- Quarterly: Minimum for active wealth builders.
- Annually: At minimum — especially useful for year-over-year comparison.

## Tools for Tracking
- **Spreadsheet**: Full control, manual updates.
- **Personal Capital (Empower)**: Free, syncs all accounts, excellent net worth graph.
- **Mint**: Free, broader budgeting + net worth.
- **YNAB**: Budget-focused but includes net worth.

## Why Negative Net Worth Is Normal (and Temporary)
Recent graduates often have negative net worth due to student loans. This is expected — the degree increased earning potential. Focus on trajectory: is net worth improving? Even going from -$30,000 to -$10,000 is $20,000 of progress.

## The Wealth Building Formula
Net worth grows by:
1. Earning more (increasing income)
2. Spending less (increasing savings rate)
3. Investing wisely (compounding returns)
4. Avoiding wealth destroyers (high-interest debt, bad decisions)

Savings rate is the most controllable lever. A 30-year-old saving 20% of income will retire far wealthier than one saving 5%, regardless of investment returns.
